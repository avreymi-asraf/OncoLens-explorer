## Round 1 · 2026-11-11 · probes, detectors, three planted runs
* **Tried:** `oncolens/probes.py` and `oncolens/detect.py` (new), events in `runlog.py`, the loop calling both and writing sparse rows (`track.log_every`), `watch.py` with sparklines, the layer table, events, `analyze` and `--report`, `configs/observe.toml`. Commit `94eeec7`.
* **Run plan:** Question: do the tools name a planted fault while the run is going, without being told what was planted, and stay quiet otherwise? Three runs on Colab CPU with `configs/observe.toml`: `02-overfit` (the default, 60,000 steps, known from experiment 00 to overfit), `03-too-hot` (`train.lr=3.0`, 20,000 steps), `04-too-cold` (`train.lr=1e-8`, 20,000 steps). Watch: `oncolens.watch` on the mirrored folder two or three times mid-run; the events as they are raised. Stop early: not used here on purpose; the runs go to the end so that an event can be checked against everything that happened after it.
* **Saw:**
  * Before Colab, locally: the detectors run on experiment 00's run `02-colab` (made before they existed) and name its overfitting (best at step 700). Probes cost about 13% of speed (1,350 → 1,170 steps/s, 20,000 steps, no W&B, session machine).
  * `02-overfit`: one event, raised inside the run at step 2,000 of 60,000 (about 5 seconds into a 130 second run): validation loss best at step 500 (0.461), 0.533 at step 2,000 while training loss fell. Read mid-run at steps 225, 18,450 and 38,650. Nothing was raised before step 2,000. What the probes add to "it overfits": validation AUROC does not move (0.794 at step 500, 0.794 at step 60,000) and the share of validation patients on the right side barely moves (0.79 → 0.77); what collapses is confidence. Calibration error goes 0.036 → 0.204, the median per-patient validation loss goes to 0.000 while the 90th percentile goes 1.2 → 7.6: a tenth of the patients are being predicted wrongly with near certainty. On the training side 99.3% are right at the end, with 2,200 right-then-wrong flips along the way. 58,000 of the 60,000 steps came after the event.
  * `03-too-hot`: the whole failure, in order, in the first second: every layer changed by 100% of its size at step 1; training loss 2,757 times its first value at step 25; 95% and 88% of the hidden units dead at the first evaluation (step 250); all layers frozen (zero gradient) from step 150; "nothing is being learned" at step 1,500. Read mid-run at steps 2,350 and 12,125.
  * `04-too-cold`: all layers "effectively frozen since step 1" (updates about 8e-08 of the layer per step), raised at step 200; "nothing is being learned" at step 1,500. Read mid-run at steps 2,775 and 15,725.
  * Run files: `runs/02-overfit/`, `runs/03-too-hot/`, `runs/04-too-cold/`, each with `events.jsonl` and `analysis.md`.
* **Found by watching, and fixed (commit after `94eeec7`):**
  * **The shared `auroc` was wrong on ties.** `03-too-hot` gives every patient the same score and reported validation AUROC 0.0099; the right value is 0.5. Ties now count as half (checked against a brute-force count). Experiment 00's values were not visibly affected (continuous scores, few ties). `04-too-cold` ran with the fix; `03-too-hot`'s `metrics.jsonl` still holds the wrong 0.0099.
  * The frozen-layer event recomputed from outside named a later start step than the one raised in the run (it looked at the last five probes only). It now names the start of the frozen stretch, the same from inside and outside.
  * Activations were keyed by the ReLU module (`body.1`) and not by the layer they belong to (`body.0`). Renamed in the probes; runs `02` and `03` keep the old keys, so their `analysis.md` shows "n/a" for dead units.
* **Not seen, said plainly:** a run with no fault at all (this toy always overfits), so "stays quiet on a healthy run" rests on the 2,000 steps before the event in `02-overfit` only. A GPU run. A fault that develops slowly (a late divergence, a slow leak). Thresholds are constants chosen on this toy.
* **Decided:** The stopping point is reached: three planted runs, each fault named mid-run, cost known. Closing. The detectors were not tuned further (plan: thresholds fitted to this toy say little about later models).
* **Told the lab:** final conclusion: closing. Claims `detectors-name-planted-faults-mid-run` and `overfit-toy-is-confidence-not-ranking`; findings page `watching-a-run`; system page (probes, detectors, events, new extension point, AUROC fix).
